About

Breadth after depth.

My father became a mechanical engineer without a college degree, teaching himself from library books. He gave me the two things I still love most: basketball and technology. His love of maths is why I studied engineering at NIT Warangal.

We moved constantly. I've lived in 21 cities, and in each one basketball was how I found my footing. I went on to play at the national level, captained my college team, and still coach when I can.

At Fidelity I started as a software engineer in Investment Compliance and grew into leading product for Global Cash Operations. After six years deep in one domain, I wanted range. I came to Fuqua to move from a techno-functional manager to a strategic leader who can take on bigger problems, and I'm recruiting for consulting with that goal in mind.

LedgerGuard

Co-founder · India · Early stage

GST input tax credit, reconciled and defensible

Indian businesses lose input tax credit when their purchase records don't match what suppliers file. LedgerGuard reconciles the two, explains every mismatch, and gives Chartered Accountant firms a sign-off pack they can stand behind in an audit. We're starting with CA firms and have our first beta client.

Visit LedgerGuard →

  1. Pull the purchase register and the GSTR-2B portal record
  2. Match invoices and flag every mismatch
  3. Root-cause each gap and route the fix to the vendor
  4. Track Section 16(4) deadlines and blocked credits
  5. Produce a CA sign-off and audit-defense pack
